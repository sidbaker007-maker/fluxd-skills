## 2. Metaphor Discovery & Word-Mapping Protocol

Never jump straight from a company name to drawing a literal icon. Follow this 3-step divergence protocol first:

1. **Write the Positioning Sentence**:
   `"For [target audience], [Brand Name] is the [category] that [core differentiator / emotional promise]."`
2. **Build a 3-Column Word Map**:
   - *Column A (Tangible Objects & Architecture)*: Physical artifacts, tools, architectural structures, or natural forms associated with the craft (e.g., for a roaster: kiln arch, roasting drum, chaff, burlap weave).
   - *Column B (Actions, Mechanics & Geometry)*: Verbs and spatial behaviors (e.g., converge, filter, hand-off, refract, dock, pivot, weave, interlock).
   - *Column C (Brand Initials & Letterform Anatomy)*: The structural features of the first letter or key glyphs in the name (e.g., the vertex of `M`, the counter of `b`, the crossbar of `T`, the ampersand `&`).
3. **Cross-Pollinate 8–12 One-Line Concepts, Select Top 3**:
   Combine an item from Column A or B with Column C (or combine two unexpected tangible forms into a dual-meaning silhouette). Filter the 8–12 one-liners down to **3 structurally distinct concepts** (e.g., Direction A: Geometric Lettermark; Direction B: Dual-Meaning Pictorial Symbol; Direction C: Negative-Space or Mascot-Feature Mark).

## 3. The 7 Mark Archetypes & When to Use Each

1. **Custom Wordmark / Logotype**: The full brand name set in bespoke or structurally modified letterforms. Best when the name is short (4–8 letters), distinctive, and needs maximum name recognition.
2. **Lettermark / Monogram**: Built from the brand's initial(s) with a metaphorical cut, counter, or negative-space reveal (e.g., a `K` cut like kite panels, or a `B` whose counters form an arched warehouse doorway). Best for versatile app icons and favicons.
3. **Pictorial Symbol (Dual-Meaning)**: A recognizable physical object drawn with extreme geometric restraint that carries a second meaning (e.g., an open book whose ribbon forms a `T`, or a round sourdough loaf whose score lifts into a lark's crest).
4. **Abstract / Geometric Mark**: Constructed from pure geometric primitives (circles, clean 45°/60° diagonals, rotational symmetry) expressing motion, connection, or structure. Best for enterprise infrastructure, conglomerates, and technical platforms.
5. **Negative-Space Mark**: Uses the background canvas carved out between solid ink shapes to reveal a hidden letter or secondary symbol. Creates an immediate "aha" moment and prints effortlessly in a single ink.
6. **Mascot / Feature-Extracted Mark**: Reduces an animal, character, or celestial entity to one iconic silhouette feature (see Section 4). Best for consumer apps, gaming, food, veterinary, and community brands.
7. **Architectural Emblem / Tile**: A self-contained badge or repeating tile geometry where the border and interior lock together. Ensure interior gaps remain wide enough to survive at `24px`.

## 4. Mascot & Feature-Extraction Rules

Inspired by `SanbaoAI/logo-generator-skill` (`mascot-logo-generator`), when a brand calls for a character, animal, or organic mascot:

- **Extract One Signature Feature, Not a Full Illustration**: Full-body cartoons with thin outlines collapse into blobs at `16px`. Instead, isolate one unmistakable anatomical or structural feature:
  - *Examples*: A pointed cat ear paired with a floppy dog ear (`Bramble Vet`); a crescent moon shadow cutout; a koi tail split; a perched finch inside an ampersand; antlers; bear paw pads; a smiling citrus wedge.
- **Flat Silhouette Compression**: Construct the mascot mark using 1 to 2 solid flat shapes with generous negative-space cutouts so it stamps cleanly in a single color on kraft paper, embroidery, or a `16x16` favicon.
- **Honest Misread Check**: Explicitly test whether the simplified silhouette could be misread as an unintended object (e.g., a wheel looking like a donut, a star-fort looking like a shuriken, or a citrus wedge looking like watermelon) and refine the geometry to eliminate the ambiguity.

## 5. SVG Geometry, Optical Corrections & Craft Rules

Every vector logo generated by this skill must obey strict SVG engineering discipline and pass `scripts/brand_audit.py`:

### Structural SVG Standards
- **Normalized Canvas**: Use `viewBox="0 0 256 256"` for square symbols/icons and `viewBox="0 0 800 256"` (or `0 0 900 256`) for horizontal wordmark lockups.
- **Anchor Point Economy**: Keep total path anchor points lean—target **under 120 anchor points** for a symbol (the median across 1,400+ iconic world logos is 53–75 points; anything above 155 points signals over-complicated paths or unmerged shapes).
- **Clean Angle Discipline**: Snap diagonal strokes and polygon edges to deliberate geometric angles (`30°`, `45°`, `60°`, `75°`, `90°`). Avoid accidental near-miss angles like `43.7°` or `88.9°`.
- **Minimum Feature & Gap Floor**: No sub-shape, stroke thickness, or negative-space gap may be smaller than **1/48th of the canvas** ($\ge 5.5$ units on a `256x256` viewBox). Any gap narrower than 5.5 units will plug up when rendered at `16px` or stamped in ink.

### Four Classical Optical Corrections
1. **Overshoot**: Circles, arches, and pointed apexes (`A`, `V`, `M`, triangles) look smaller than flat rectangles of the same mathematical height. Extend curved tops/bottoms by `1.5%–2%` (`3–5` units on a 256 canvas) past the cap/baseline guide.
2. **Irradiation Compensation**: A white mark on a black background appears optically fatter than the exact same black mark on a white background due to light bleed on the retina. Keep internal negative-space gaps generous (`6–10` units) so the inverted mark doesn't close up.
3. **Mass & Visual Center**: Geometric center is not optical center. A triangle or directional play-arrow centered by bounding box looks shifted toward its heavy base; shift its centroid slightly toward the weighted side until it balances visually inside a circular avatar and rounded-square app icon.
4. **Join & Stroke Uniformity**: When combining strokes and filled shapes, keep stroke weights mathematically consistent (e.g., all strokes `16` or `20` units wide) and use `stroke-linecap="round"` or clean mitered joins intentionally—never mix arbitrary stroke widths.

## 6. Brand-System Board Architecture & 12 Showcase Environments

A logo on a blank white square is never a finished deliverable. Inspired by `SanbaoAI/logo-generator-skill`, `kaankiziltug/logo-design-skill`, and `Collective Brain Brandkit`, present the approved identity as a **Numbered 8-Module Brand-System Board**:

1. **Module 01 — Primary Lockup & Positioning**: Horizontal lockup (symbol + customized wordmark) paired with the one-sentence positioning statement.
2. **Module 02 — Symbol Construction & Metaphor Grid**: Geometric construction overlay showing the underlying grid ratios, angles, and metaphorical meaning diagram.
3. **Module 03 — Small-Size Pixel Ladder (`64px` / `32px` / `16px`)**: Live rendered scale test proving the mark survives as a browser tab favicon and tiny UI badge.
4. **Module 04 — Surface Polarity Matrix**: 4-way lockup test: Primary on Light Canvas, Inverted on Dark Ink, Signature Color Stage, and 1-Color Monochrome Stamp.
5. **Module 05 — Contrast-Verified Color System**: Swatch cards for all 5–6 brand colors displaying HEX, RGB, semantic role, 60-30-10 share, and live WCAG contrast badges (`AAA` / `AA` / `Large Only`).
6. **Module 06 — Typographic Hierarchy & Specimen**: Live rendered Display/Headline font, Body font, and Monospace/Label tokens with modular scale sizes (`H1` to `Caption`), tracking, and line-height specs.
7. **Module 07 — Industry-Specific Application Mockups**: 3 contextual real-world touchpoints tailored to the sector (e.g., App Icon + SaaS UI Header + Social Avatar for software; Packaging Label + Embossed Business Card + Storefront Sign for physical goods).
8. **Module 08 — Usage Guardrails ("The 3 Don'ts") & Asset Manifest**: Explicit anti-patterns (no luminance vibration pairings, no unapproved gradients/drop-shadows, minimum clear-space rule) and downloadable file links.

### 12 Showcase Lighting & Material Styles (For AI Image Generation via `image_gen`)

When using `image_gen:generate_image` to render photorealistic brand showcase boards or physical mockups, select 1–2 lighting/material environments from `op7418/logo-generator-skill` that match the brand's positioning:

- **Dark Environments**:
  1. *Void*: Matte carbon minimalism with crisp edge rim-lighting (hardcore tech, developer tools).
  2. *Frosted Dome*: Smoked acrylic glass diffusion over subtle geometric glow (modern SaaS, fintech).
  3. *Fluid Abyss*: Deep organic dark silk / liquid refraction (AI platforms, audio, creative tools).
  4. *Studio Spotlight*: Dramatic architectural key light casting a soft directional shadow on slate (luxury, sports).
  5. *Analog Liquid*: Brushed anodized metal or chrome deboss on deep pigment (industrial, hardware).
  6. *LED Matrix*: Tactile dot-matrix / phosphor grid glow (retro-futurism, gaming, developer CLI).
- **Light Environments**:
  7. *Editorial Paper*: Uncoated 350gsm cotton cardstock with subtle blind deboss and warm studio light (coffee, publishing, architecture, boutique agencies).
  8. *Iridescent Frosted*: Translucent alabaster glass with soft optical refraction (skincare, wellness, modern consumer).
  9. *Morning Mist*: Soft diffused daylight over warm plaster and natural travertine (hospitality, lifestyle).
  10. *Clinical Studio*: High-key orthographic grid with crisp matte ceramic surfaces (healthtech, biotech, pharmacy).
  11. *UI Container*: Tactile bento card elevation with crisp hairline borders (product ecosystems, mobile apps).
  12. *Swiss Flat*: Pure poster print with bold ink registration on warm newsprint (festivals, cultural brands, design systems).
