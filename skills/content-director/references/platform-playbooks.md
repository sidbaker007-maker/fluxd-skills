## 1. Format 1: YouTube 16:9 Long-Form Video Essay (12–30 Min)

### A. Packaging & CTR Blueprint (Always Design Before Scripting)
Never write a long-form script without locking the **Title + Thumbnail + First 30 Seconds** triad:
- **3 Title Archetypes**:
  1. *The Empirical Anomaly*: `"Why [Familiar Film/System] Breaks [Physical Law / Economic Rule]"`
  2. *The Geopolitical/Socio-Economic Stakes*: `"How [Micro Object/Scene] Explains India's [Macro Economic Shift]"`
  3. *The Definitive Cinephile/Science Thesis*: `"The Hidden Physics of [Film/Phenomenon]"`
- **Thumbnail Composition Spec**:
  - Maximum **3 visual elements** (1 high-contrast foreground subject/frame, 1 data curve or physical schematic overlay, 1 focal background).
  - Thumbnail text: **2–4 words maximum** in bold condensed type that *complements* (never repeats) the video title.
- **First 30 Seconds (Cold Open Retention Lock)**:
  - **0:00–0:08**: Open directly inside a specific film frame, physical paradox, or hard economic number. Zero throat-clearing (*never* say "In this video we will explore...").
  - **0:08–0:20**: Falsify the viewer's intuitive assumption with a counter-intuitive data point or physical law.
  - **0:20–0:35**: Trigger the **Telescoping Scale Jump**—pull back from the single frame or street-level detail to the global/cosmic thesis, followed by `[AUDIO PAUSE: 2s]` for the title card drop.

### B. The 6-Movement Narrative Architecture
1. **Movement I — The Inciting Anomaly (0:00–2:30)**: Cold open inside a concrete scene or physical artifact -> Premise Inversion -> Title Card.
2. **Movement II — The Surface Myth (2:30–6:00)**: State the conventional narrative (pop-science explanation, mainstream film take, or headline economic claim) and show exactly where its math or logic fails.
3. **Movement III — The Empirical Investigation (6:00–12:00)**: Introduce the primary dataset or physical formalism (e.g., RBI/MOSPI series, GDELT tone shifts, Lagrangian/thermodynamic constraint, Cinemetrics ASL distribution) rendered via `motion-designer` telemetry/math scenes.
4. **Movement IV — Cinematic / Physical Anatomy (12:00–18:00)**: Frame-by-frame or mechanism-by-mechanism breakdown showing how the director's lens/editing or the physical/economic system executes the tension.
5. **Movement V — The Systemic Diagnosis (18:00–23:00)**: Execute the **McPhee Pivot**—braid the micro case study back into the macro geopolitical, Indian socio-economic, or astrophysical reality.
6. **Movement VI — Philosophical Resolution (23:00–End)**: Land on a lasting human or cosmic insight without moralizing or begging for subscriptions.

## 2. Format 2: Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts (9:16 Vertical, 45–120 Sec)

Short-form vertical video is not a "trimmed YouTube video"—it requires vertical visual density, rapid beat changes every 3–5 seconds, and a seamless loop.

### The 5-Stage Vertical Retention Spine
1. **Stage 1 — The 0–3s Pattern Interrupt Hook (0:00–0:03)**:
   - First spoken sentence must be **<= 12 words** and pair a visual shock (a specific movie cut, a stark map, or a physical anomaly) with a blunt claim.
   - Include an **On-Screen Text Hook (`<= 6 words`)** placed in the upper-center `9:16` safe zone (avoiding Instagram UI overlays in the bottom 25% and right 15%).
2. **Stage 2 — The Myth & Friction (0:03–0:15)**:
   - State what everyone assumes about the movie, economy, or physics phenomenon, then cut it down with one hard number.
3. **Stage 3 — The Visual / Scientific Pivot (0:15–0:40)**:
   - Switch visual mode (e.g., from film clip to `motion-designer` `"kind": "math"` or `"kind": "collage"` cutout diagram) and explain the single governing mechanism.
4. **Stage 4 — The Empirical Proof (0:40–0:65)**:
   - Show the real-world consequence (in Indian/global economics, geopolitics, or cinema craft) with kinetic verb-first pacing.
5. **Stage 5 — The Seamless Loop Close (Last 5 Seconds)**:
   - Write the final sentence so its cadence or trailing clause bridges directly back into the **0:00 hook sentence** when the Reel auto-loops.

### Instagram Metadata Pack (Always Include for Reels)
- **Cover Frame Spec**: `9:16` frame with a `4:5` center-crop safe zone so it looks clean on both the Reels tab and the main profile grid.
- **Caption Structure**: 1-line curiosity hook above the fold + 3 bullet takeaways + 1 polarizing discussion question to drive comments (Instagram's primary algorithmic signal alongside Saves and Shares).
- **Pinned Comment**: A bonus nerdy detail (e.g., the exact physics equation, film lens spec, or RBI dataset link) that rewards viewers who open the comments.

## 3. Format 3: Instagram 4:5 Carousel Deep-Dive (7–10 Slides)

Instagram Carousels (`1080x1350`, `4:5` aspect ratio) drive the highest **Save Rate** for analytical creators in cinema, science, and socio-economics.

- **Slide 1 (Poster Hook)**: High-contrast visual + bold headline (`<= 8 words`) + subtle subtitle setting up the paradox.
- **Slide 2 (The Setup / Scene)**: The specific film frame, historical moment, or economic chart that reveals the anomaly (`<= 30 words`).
- **Slides 3–5 (The Mechanism)**: Step-by-step breakdown of the physics law, lens/editing technique, or socio-economic data flow (1 core diagram or frame comparison per slide, `<= 35 words` per slide).
- **Slides 6–7 (The India / Global / Cosmic Zoom-Out)**: Why this mechanism matters at scale (`<= 35 words` per slide).
- **Slide 8 (Summary Card & Save Prompt)**: A compact cheat-sheet graphic or synthesis table designed specifically so viewers tap **Bookmark / Save**.

## 4. The 1-to-4 Cross-Platform Repurposing Engine

Whenever the user asks for a full content package (or doesn't restrict to a single format), provide a **1-to-4 Cross-Platform Repurposing Plan** from the same research core:
1. **1x YouTube Long-Form Anchor (`16:9`, 15–25 min)**: The full 6-movement investigative essay.
2. **Reel #1 — The Cinema/Visual Hook (`9:16`, 60–75s)**: Extracted from Movement I & IV (focusing on the film scene or optical/physical illusion).
3. **Reel #2 — The Data/Geopolitical Shock (`9:16`, 60–75s)**: Extracted from Movement III & V (focusing on the Indian/global socio-economic or astrophysical anomaly).
4. **1x Instagram Carousel (`4:5`, 8 Slides)**: Distilling the core diagrams, physics equations, and film frames into a saveable visual thread.
