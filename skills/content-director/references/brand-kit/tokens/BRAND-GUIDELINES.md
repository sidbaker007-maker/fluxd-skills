# FLUXD (@fluxd) — Official Brand Identity Guidelines

## 1. Brand Positioning & Core Pillars
- **Positioning**: For intellectually curious viewers tired of shallow pop-science and plot summaries, **FLUXD** is the investigative video journal that braids daily cinema craft, M.Sc.-level physics rigor, geopolitics, and street-level Indian & global socio-economics into visceral visual stories.
- **Four Core Pillars**:
  1. **Cinema & Television Craft** (Anamorphic optics, focal length compression, blocking, ASL, sensitometry)
  2. **M.Sc. Physics & Astrophysics** (Flux $\Phi = \iint \mathbf{F}\cdot d\mathbf{A}$, Lagrangians, phase space, radiative transfer)
  3. **Geopolitics & Strategic Systems** (Maritime chokepoints, EUV lithography, supply-chain bottlenecks)
  4. **Socio-Economic Issues in India & the World** (RBI DBIE, MOSPI PLFS/CPI, UPI network physics)

## 2. Locked Logo System (100/100 SVG Craft Score)
- **Primary Symbol (`01-logos-svg/logo-mark-phi2-*.svg`)**: **Chamfered-Bracket `Φ-2`** — Two 45°-chamfered octagonal brackets flanking a bold vertical Flux pillar (`#E04826`). Use for YouTube/Instagram circular avatars, `16px` favicons, and video corner bugs.
- **Standalone Framed Wordmark (`01-logos-svg/logo-wordmark-framed-*.svg`)**: **`[ FLUXD ]`** — Bold geometric uppercase `FLUXD` framed inside two rectangular viewfinder brackets (`[ ]` in `#E04826`). Use for full-screen YouTube cold-open title cards and Carousel headers.
- **Combined Master Lockup (`01-logos-svg/logo-primary-lockup-*.svg`)**: Pairs the Chamfered-Bracket `Φ-2` symbol on the left with `[ FLUXD ]` on the right.
